How to Paste into Google Docs:
  1. Click "Copy for Google Docs" above.
  2. In your Google Doc, click the + button on the left sidebar to add a new tab named testing.
  3. Click inside the document and press Ctrl + V.
  4. All colors, bolding, and exact 11pt Arial styles are instantly applied!
